## 3. Data Subject Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA)

Users maintain full control over their personal data, including rights to:
- **Access & Portability**: Request a copy of all stored personal records.
- **Correction**: Update profile and preference information in-app.
- **Deletion (Erasure)**: Invoke self-serve account deletion under **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.
- **Withdrawal of Consent**: Manage optional notifications and analytics preferences.
